## Refund Timeline
Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days after the returned item passes inspection. The inspection process typically takes 1-2 business days.
For Prime and Premium members, refunds start at the pickup scan and are credited within 2-3 business days.

## Refund Methods
Refunds can be issued to the original payment method or directly to your ShopSmart Wallet. Refunds to the ShopSmart Wallet are instant.
