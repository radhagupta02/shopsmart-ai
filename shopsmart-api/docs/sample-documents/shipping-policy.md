## Delivery Speeds
Standard delivery takes 3-5 business days. Express delivery takes 1-2 business days.

## Shipping Costs by Tier
- Basic members get free standard shipping on orders of 50 USD or more; otherwise, it is 4.99 USD. Express shipping costs 9.99 USD.
- Prime members get free standard delivery on all orders, and express shipping is discounted to 4.99 USD.
- Premium members get free express delivery on all orders.

## Order Cancellation
Cancellation is free before shipment. After shipment, an order cannot be cancelled, so you must refuse delivery or return it within the standard return window.
