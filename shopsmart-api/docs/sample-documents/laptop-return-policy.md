## Unopened Products
You can return unopened products within 30 days for a full refund.

## Opened Products
Opened products are returnable within 7 days of delivery if there is no physical damage. The product must be returned with all original accessories, its original packaging, and after a factory reset.

## Damaged or Defective Products
If the product is defective on arrival, you are eligible for a replacement or a full refund within 15 days.
Please note that customer-caused physical damage is not returnable, though warranty conditions may apply.
